AccessFlows

Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

AccessFlows is a local-first application. What you send and what comes back stays on your machine. This page says exactly what leaves it, and only when you sign in or share something.

1. What stays on your computer

Collections, requests, responses, headers, environment variables, history and your settings are stored locally, in the application's own store. We do not run a proxy in the middle of your API traffic: requests go from your machine to the endpoint you named, and we never see them or the responses.

2. What we hold when you have an account

An account needs an email address, and an account is only created if you choose to make one.

  • Email address — to sign you in, confirm the address, and reset a password.
  • Account identifier — a random id that ties your shares, your plan and your settings to you.
  • Sign-in provider — whether the account signs in with a password, Google, GitHub or Microsoft. With a provider we receive the address and the identifier it gives us, never a password.
  • Profile you choose to set — a display name and an avatar, if you add them.
  • Plan and subscription — which plan the account is on, how many seats, and the store's identifiers for the subscription. Not your card.
  • A business verification, if you file one — the business details you typed, the documents you uploaded, and the version of the terms you accepted.

3. What you share is stored, and for whom

A collection you share is uploaded so the people you shared it with can open it, together with the name and description you gave it. A private share is readable by the people you named, and a share to the Community is readable by anyone with the link and appears in the directory.

Unsharing deletes the stored copy shortly after. A revoked business verification hides the Community shares of that account and deletes them 30 days later, unless it is restored first. Deleting your account removes everything of yours that our servers hold.

4. Payments

Payments are taken by Creem, which is the merchant of record. The card, the billing address and the invoice live in Creem's systems under its own privacy policy, and the tax it collects is handled there. We keep the subscription's identifier, its state and the plan it carries — what the application needs to know which plan is active.

5. Who processes data for us

  • Supabase — accounts, the database and the storage that holds shared collections.
  • Creem — checkout, payments, invoices and tax.
  • GitHub — the static pages you are reading now, which carry no scripts and no analytics.
  • The sign-in provider you chose, and only for the sign-in itself.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent data, and there is no advertising network anywhere in the product.
  • We do not track the API traffic you generate.
  • We do not read your collections for any purpose other than hosting what you published, and reviewing a business verification you filed.

7. Security

Accounts and shares are guarded by row-level rules: a share is served only to the account that owns it and to the people it was shared with, and a business document is readable only by the desk reviewing it. Locally, the security of your data is the security of your machine — keep it patched and keep the app's own store out of folders you sync publicly.

8. How long things are kept

  • The account, until you delete it.
  • A shared collection, until you unshare it or delete the account.
  • Business verification documents, 90 days after a decision on the request — then the row and the uploaded files are removed. Deleting the account removes them sooner.
  • A hidden Community share, 30 days after the verification was revoked, unless it is restored.

9. Your choices

You can see and change your profile in the app, unshare anything you published, and delete the whole account from the account settings — which removes the account and what our servers hold for it, and cancels the subscription. To ask for a copy of what we hold, or to correct something the app will not let you change, open an issue on the AccessFlows repository.

10. Children

The application is a tool for building and testing APIs and is not directed at children. An account is for someone old enough to enter into a contract where they live.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change that widens what we collect is announced in the application before it takes effect.

One thing worth saying plainly: the copy of your collections on your machine is yours and is never uploaded unless you share it. If you lose that machine, we cannot restore it for you.
