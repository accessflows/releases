### Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 09, 2026** 

This Privacy Policy governs your use of our open-source/free API client application (the "Application") for Windows devices. 

By installing and using the Application, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy. 

### 1. Data Collection and Usage

The Application is designed to operate primarily as a local tool. 

* **Local Storage:** All API requests, responses, headers, environment variables, collection data, and history are stored locally on your machine.
* **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, store, or share any personal identifiable information (PII).
* **No Server-Side Tracking:** We do not track your API traffic, and we do not host any remote servers that intercept your data.

### 2. Third-Party Services

Since the Application is used to send HTTP/HTTPS requests to third-party APIs designated by you, your data is subject to the privacy policies of those specific third-party API providers. We highly recommend reviewing the privacy policies of the endpoints you interact with. 

### 3. Data Security

Because all your data is stored locally on your Windows device, the security of your data depends entirely on the security of your local environment and device. We do not have access to your local database or configuration files. 

### 4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
